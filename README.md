## My projects

### 1. Retail Sales Analysis
My individual portfolio project. I analysed a fictional retail dataset from January to March 2026, using Excel, SQL, and Python. I verified the headline results myself: AUD 3,378 revenue, AUD 1,554 profit, 199 units, and a 46.0% overall margin. I also compared categories, cities, products, and months, and wrote up what the differences actually mean. Full details are in `01-retail-sales-analysis/`.

### 2. Breast Cancer Diagnostic Classification (WDBC)
I compared five classification models on the Wisconsin diagnostic dataset, on the same unseen test set, with feature selection learned only from training data to avoid leakage. Logistic Regression had the highest test-set F1. Full details are in `02-wdbc-breast-cancer/`.

### 4. IT Service Management — ITIL 4 (Group Project)
